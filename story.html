The $2.3M Deal That Almost Died in Month 9

In 2014, a Ukrainian tech company hired a Big Four consulting firm to enter Kazakhstan. The firm produced a 200-page market-entry strategy. It took 11 months. The client paid $180,000. When they landed in Almaty, they discovered the regulatory map was wrong, the local partner introductions were cold, and the bilingual execution team didn't exist.

The deal died. Not because the strategy was bad. Because 11 months of analysis couldn't survive 11 days of reality on the ground.

"I watched that deal collapse from across the table. That's when I realized: emerging markets don't reward the firm with the best deck. They reward the operator who picks up the phone, speaks the language, and shows up on Monday morning with a team that actually lives there."

— The operator behind Sipiteno

Sipiteno was built on that lesson. We don't write 200-page decks. We make warm introductions to people already trusted inside the market. We build regulatory maps from 15 years of operating, not from desktop research. And we put bilingual teams on the ground in 4-8 weeks, not 11 months.

We aim to get a first deal signed inside a quarter. That's the target we design engagements around, not a measured average — timelines vary by market, sector and how warm your starting network is.
