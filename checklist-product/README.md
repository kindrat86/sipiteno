## The 12 points

### 1. Holiday order cutoff
Add as an explicit fact: "Orders placed after [DATE/TIME] ship in January." Agents cannot infer this from shipping policies.

### 2. Return window in days
"The store accepts returns within [N] days of delivery." A number, not "see policy". Generic answers lose agent sales.

### 3. Return shipping cost
Who pays: the store or the customer. Agents quote this before checkout.

### 4. Shipping destinations
List countries/zones you serve. Delete zones you stopped serving (old zones still read as "yes").

### 5. Shipping cost and delivery estimate
Per zone: flat or calculated, and the range ("3-5 business days after processing").

### 6. Processing time
How long between order and ship. Without this, an agent's delivery estimate is wrong even with a correct range.

### 7. Payment options
Cards, wallets, BNPL - name them. Agents answer "can I pay with X" from this.

### 8. Gift services
Gift wrap, gift notes, no-price receipts. December buyers ask.

### 9. Warranty and guarantees
Warranty length, satisfaction guarantee, what it covers.

### 10. Contact and support facts
Support hours, response time, email. Agents answer "how do I reach a human".

### 11. Old seasonal pages
Search your storefront for last year's holiday page and retired policy pages. Anything published still reads as true to an agent. Unpublish or update.

### 12. Top-20 product titles as facts
Rewrite titles of your 20 best sellers so an agent can quote material, size, use case without opening the page.
