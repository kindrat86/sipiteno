The problem

Admin API tokens for public apps are long-lived secrets. Without an IP allowlist, a token stolen from a log file, a repo, or a client-side bundle works from any server on the internet. The allowlist changes that: your tokens only work from the addresses or networks you list.

Most app teams have drift between what the code does and what the allowlist says: background jobs on one platform, webhooks on another, a queue worker in a third region. Configure the allowlist wrong and you break your own app hours before the biggest sales weeks of the year. That is the engineering problem we solve - on a deadline you do not control.

On serverless or dynamic IPs? A single static IP will not cut it. Shopify supports allowlisting by network (ASN) exactly for this case - that is the path we configure.

FAQ

Is Sipiteno affiliated with Shopify?

No. We are an independent engineering consultancy. Everything here follows Shopify's public developer changelog, linked above.

What happens if we miss 12 November (bulk ops)?

Bulk queries and mutations from your public app are blocked until an allowlist is configured. Regular Admin API calls keep working until the later deadlines. Apps using bulk operations get priority in our queue.

We are on serverless with no stable IP. Is that a problem?

No. Shopify supports allowlisting by network with ASN support, built for serverless hosting. That is the Serverless or Rush tier.

Do you need access to merchant data or our stores?

No. This is Partner Dashboard configuration and your own infrastructure. We never touch merchant data.
