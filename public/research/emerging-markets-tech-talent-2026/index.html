Methodology

Data compiled from:

  • LinkedIn talent searches (filtered by country + "software developer" / "AI engineer" / "data scientist")
  • Stack Overflow Developer Survey 2024-2025 regional compensation data
  • World Bank GDP and ease-of-doing-business indicators
  • OECD tax database for corporate tax rates
  • Numbeo cost-of-living index (normalized to 0-100, lower = cheaper)
  • 15+ years of Sipiteno's field experience operating across all 28 countries

All figures are estimates based on the best available public data. Salary figures are mid-range for senior developers in USD, adjusted for local cost of living.

Key Findings

1. Ukraine has the deepest talent pool per capita

With 200,000+ developers (1 per ~200 population), Ukraine ranks highest in developer density. Poland has the absolute largest pool at 400,000+, but also a larger population.

2. Bulgaria and Georgia offer the lowest operational costs

Bulgaria's 10% flat corporate tax and Georgia's 0% IT zone rate make them the most cost-effective bases. However, EU membership (Bulgaria) provides regulatory certainty that Georgia's simplified regime trades off.

3. The salary gap is narrowing

Senior developer salaries in Poland and Czech Republic have risen 30-40% since 2022, approaching Western European levels. Ukraine, Georgia, and Kazakhstan remain 50-70% cheaper for comparable talent.

Implications for Founders

Best for cost-to-talent ratio:

Ukraine and Bulgaria — large pools at competitive rates. The trade-off: Ukraine has geopolitical risk; Bulgaria has smaller pool.

Best for EU regulatory compliance:

Poland, Romania, Czech Republic — full EU membership means GDPR alignment, IP protection, and seamless intra-EU operations.

Best for speed of setup:

Georgia — company registration in 1 business day, 0% tax for IT companies in the Virtual Zone. Ideal for testing market entry before committing to EU incorporation.
