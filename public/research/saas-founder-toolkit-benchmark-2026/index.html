Introduction

What tools do solo founders actually use — not what the marketing sites say, but what real builders open every day? This report aggregates community-sourced data from founder communities to benchmark 25 tools across 11 categories.

The data reveals a clear pattern: founders optimize for free or low-cost tier availability first, user experience second. The tools with the highest satisfaction ratings (VS Code, Stripe, Figma, Linear) are also the ones founders most actively recommend to peers — suggesting that quality compounds through word-of-mouth.

Methodology

This data was curated from aggregated community responses shared in public founder communities (Indie Hackers, Hacker News "Ask HN: What tools do you use?" threads, r/SaaS, and X/Twitter founder polls conducted between April and June 2026). The dataset represents estimated aggregate figures based on observed patterns, not a formal statistical survey.

Adoption percentages represent the share of solo founders who reported using each tool at least weekly. Ratings are median user satisfaction scores shared in community posts and reviews. Tool categories are self-assigned, meaning a tool like Notion may appear under both "Project Management" and "Documentation" depending on how founders reported using it.

Data disclaimer: These figures are estimates based on publicly shared community responses, not proprietary survey data. Individual tool adoption varies by sector, geography, and business stage. Attribution should reference the community-sourced nature of this benchmark.
