How much do AI voice agents cost in 2026

Bottom Line AI voice agents cost $0.05-0.15 per call in 2026, with monthly platform fees ranging from $200-2,000 depending on call volume and features — typically 90% cheaper than human agents for routine calls.

Per-Call Pricing

Most platforms charge per minute ($0.02-0.08/min) or per call ($0.05-0.15/call). High-volume businesses (10,000+ calls/month) can negotiate rates as low as $0.02/call. This includes speech-to-text, LLM processing, and text-to-speech.

Platform Subscription Tiers

Entry-level: $200-500/month for up to 1,000 calls with basic flows. Professional: $500-1,000/month for up to 5,000 calls with custom flows and basic integrations. Enterprise: $1,000-2,000+/month for unlimited calls, custom integrations, analytics, and dedicated support.

Hidden Costs to Watch For

Phone number provisioning ($1-5/month per number), CRM integration setup ($500-2,000 one-time), custom voice model training ($1,000-5,000 one-time), and international call rates (can be 2-3x domestic rates).

ROI Calculation

For a business handling 5,000 calls/month at $3/call with human agents ($15,000/month), switching to AI at $0.10/call ($500/month) plus a $500 platform fee saves $14,000/month — a 93% reduction. The platform pays for itself in the first month.

Key Data Points

  • $0.05-0.15 per call
  • 93% cost reduction vs humans
  • $200-2,000/month platforms

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Is there a free trial?

    Most platforms offer a 14-day free trial with 100-500 test calls. Use this to evaluate voice quality, flow design capabilities, and integration depth before committing.
