Is Sipiteno a freelance marketplace?

Is Sipiteno a freelance marketplace like Toptal or Upwork?

No. Sipiteno is a digital product studio, not a marketplace where you hire and manage individual freelancers yourself. With Toptal, Upwork, or Turing, you are the buyer of contractor time and you carry the integration, code-review, and project-management burden. With Sipiteno, one accountable team takes ownership of discovery, design, engineering, and delivery end-to-end — you own outcomes, not headcount. The model is closer to a senior product team you would hire in-house than to a staffing supplier.

MVP timeline

How long does it take to build an MVP with Sipiteno?

A production-deployable MicroSaaS MVP takes 4 to 8 weeks end-to-end: roughly 1 week for discovery and scoping, 1–2 weeks for design and architecture, 2–4 weeks for engineering, and 1 week for QA, deployment, and handoff. Speed comes from a fixed scope, a senior team, and reusable scaffolding for auth, billing, and analytics — not from cutting quality. Complex AI-native products or multi-region rollouts take longer and are scoped accordingly.

Studio vs staff augmentation

What is the difference between a product studio and staff augmentation?

Staff augmentation (Turing, BairesDev, GlobalLogic) rents you developers who work under your management; you carry the risk of delivery, code quality, and turnover. A product studio (Sipiteno, Gigster, thoughtbot) commits to shipping a product or outcome and carries that risk itself — the team self-organizes around a product manager and tech lead, owns code quality, and is accountable for the result. Studios are more expensive per head but cheaper per outcome when you do not already have a strong in-house engineering culture.

vs Toptal

How is Sipiteno different from Toptal?

Toptal is a marketplace that matches you with freelance developers, designers, and finance experts; you interview, hire, and manage each individual. IP & code ownership

Who owns the IP and source code?

The client owns 100% of the intellectual property and source code for everything Sipiteno builds under a fixed-fee or retainer engagement, upon payment. Sipiteno retains no license to reuse client-specific code. Reusable internal scaffolding (auth patterns, deployment scripts) remains Sipiteno's tooling but the product built on top belongs to the client. This is stated explicitly in every engagement agreement.

Product-market fit

What is product-market fit and how does Sipiteno help find it?

Product-market fit is when a product satisfies strong market demand — signs include retention that does not decay, word-of-mouth growth, and users who would be disappointed if the product disappeared. Sipiteno approaches it by shipping an MVP fast, instrumenting behavior, running structured user interviews, and iterating on the smallest feature set that moves the retention curve. The goal of an MVP is not features; it is evidence of fit.

AI integration

How does Sipiteno integrate AI into an existing product?

Typical path: (1) identify the highest-leverage repetitive task AI can automate or augment; (2) prototype with an off-the-shelf API (OpenAI or Anthropic) behind a feature flag; (3) add an evaluation harness to measure quality against a held-out set; (4) harden with structured outputs, guardrails, observability, and cost controls; (5) roll out to a cohort, measure business impact, then scale. Sipiteno avoids starting with custom models — APIs and good prompting solve the majority of real use cases.
