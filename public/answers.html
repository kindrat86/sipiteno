Quick answers to common questions.

How to build an MVP
Build an MVP by: identifying the core problem, defining the minimum feature set, choosing the right tech stack, building fast, and iterating based on user feedback.
What is product-market fit
Product-market fit is when your product satisfies a strong market demand. Signs include: retention growth, word-of-mouth referrals, and users who would be disappointed if your product disappeared.
How to integrate AI into your product
Integrate AI by: identifying repetitive tasks AI can automate, choosing an API or model, starting with a narrow use case, and measuring impact before scaling.
