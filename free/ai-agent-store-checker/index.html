The fix is free and takes about 20 minutes: in Shopify admin, open Sales channels → Agentic → Knowledge base, add your holiday order cutoff and return window as explicit facts, approve and publish. Rescan can take up to 48 hours per Shopify's docs, so do it before Black Friday week.
