The free 12-point self-check

Walk the list yourself first - it is free and takes about 20 minutes. Tick each point you are confident about; nothing is sent anywhere, this page runs entirely in your browser.

0 / 12 points ticked. Start with point 1: the Knowledge Base.
1. Knowledge Base facts reviewed and published

Check: returns window, shipping regions and times, and gift services are filled in, correct, and published (not left as drafts).

Where: Sales channels > Agentic > Knowledge base.

Why agents care: these facts are the source of truth agents query for non-product questions; unpublished facts are invisible to them.

Docs: Shopify Help Center - Knowledge base

2. /agents.md serves accurate facts

Check: open yourstore.com/agents.md and read it. Does it state your real policies, regions and services?

Where: it is auto-generated; to control it, add a templates/agents.md.liquid theme template.

Why agents care: /agents.md is the canonical agent-discovery document for your store.

Docs: agents.md.liquid template reference

3. /llms.txt present and accurate

Check: open yourstore.com/llms.txt. It mirrors /agents.md by default - if you customized one, check the other still says something useful.

Where: same theme template family; /llms.txt and /llms-full.txt fall back to agents.md.liquid.

Why agents care: agents that request /llms.txt still find a usable document.

Docs: Customize /llms.txt and /agents.md

4. Product titles carry specifics

Check: a title should carry model, size, material or compatibility - not just the brand and a vague noun.

Where: Products in admin, or a bulk title pass.

Why agents care: agents match titles to specific customer questions ("a 300ml borosilicate carafe" beats "premium carafe").

Docs: Shopify Help Center - Products

5. Descriptions answer buyer questions

Check: dimensions, materials, care instructions and compatibility are stated in the description, in plain sentences.

Where: product description field.

Why agents care: the description is where the agent quotes from; "see images" tells it nothing.

Docs: Shopify Help Center - Products

6. Structured data: Product, Offer, policies

Check: product pages expose Product and Offer schema, and your shipping/return policy pages are reachable and linked.

Where: most current themes do this automatically - verify in Google's Rich Results test on a product URL.

Why agents care: structured data is the machine-readable version of price, availability and terms.

Docs: Google - Product structured data

7. Guest checkout is on

Check: a buyer can complete checkout without creating an account.

Where: Settings > Checkout - customer account settings.

Why agents care: forced account creation is a classic point where an agent-led purchase stalls and dies.

Docs: Shopify Help Center - Checkout

8. No SMS or password gates blocking checkout

Check: no bot walls, forced app installs, or password screens between the product and a completed order.

Where: walk your own checkout on a phone in a private window.

Why agents care: agents cannot pass interactive walls, so the purchase just fails.

Docs: Shopify Help Center - Checkout

9. Policy pages reachable

Check: refund, shipping, privacy and terms pages exist, load, and are linked in the footer.

Where: Settings > Policies, plus footer menu links.

Why agents care: agents cite and link policies when answering "can I return this?"

Docs: Shopify Help Center - Store policies

10. Reviews visible on the page

Check: product reviews render on the product page itself, not locked inside an app's popup.

Where: your reviews app's widget placement, or Shop product reviews.

Why agents care: visible reviews are facts an agent can weigh ("highly rated for sizing true to fit").

Docs: Shopify Help Center - Shop product reviews

11. Out-of-stock handling says something

Check: sold-out items are clearly marked, ideally with a restock signal or notification option - not silently purchasable or vague.

Where: product status + theme's sold-out badge; notify-me apps.

Why agents care: an agent that recommends an unavailable product loses the user's trust for the next question.

Docs: Shopify Help Center - Product variants & inventory

12. Variant names a human understands

Check: options are named "Size", "Color", "Material" - not "Option 1" / "Option 2".

Where: product options in admin.

Why agents care: "Option 1: Large" is meaningless to a shopper asking for a size L in green.

Docs: Shopify Help Center - Variants
