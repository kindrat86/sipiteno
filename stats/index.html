SaaS Industry

$300B+
Global SaaS market revenue 2025
Global SaaS market revenue exceeded $300 billion in 2025, with the market projected to reach $700B+ by 2030. The top 10 SaaS companies account for approximately 35% of total market revenue.
Source: Gartner; IDC; Synergy Research Group
APA: Gartner. (2026). SaaS Market Forecast.
~30,000
Number of SaaS companies globally
There are an estimated 30,000+ SaaS companies globally, with approximately 17,000 in the United States alone. The majority (85%) have under $10M in annual revenue.
Source: Statista; Crunchbase; industry estimates
APA: Statista. (2026). SaaS Industry Statistics.
5–7
Average number of SaaS tools per SMB
The average small-to-medium business uses 5–7 SaaS tools, with tech-forward SMBs using 15+. SaaS spend per employee averages $2,800/year and is growing at 14% annually.
Source: BetterCloud; Blissfully SaaS Trends Report
APA: BetterCloud. (2025). SaaS Trends Report.

AI agents market size 2026

Startups & Funding

$4.9T
Global M&A deal value 2025
2025 was the second-highest year on record for global M&A at $4.9 trillion, up 40% year-over-year. Technology, Media, and Telecom accounted for 28% of deal value.
Source: Bain & Company, M&A Report 2026
APA: Bain & Company. (2026). M&A Report 2026.
3–6 weeks
Engineering signal lead time before funding announcements
Analysis of ~400 startup GitHub organizations shows that engineering acceleration signals appear 3–6 weeks before public fundraise announcements, with a 72% correlation rate.
Source: GitDealFlow, VC Deal Flow Signal (SSRN 6606558)
APA: GitDealFlow. (2026). VC Deal Flow Signal. SSRN 6606558.

Business Exits

2.58x
Average small business sale multiple
The average earnings (SDE) multiple across all sectors for small business sales is 2.58x. SaaS and recurring-revenue businesses command 2–3x higher multiples than non-recurring-revenue businesses.
Source: BizBuySell; Acquire.com
APA: BizBuySell. (2026). Business Valuation Multiples by Industry.
~70%
Small businesses that never sell
An estimated 70% of small businesses listed for sale never find a buyer, primarily due to unrealistic valuations, poor documentation, and owner-dependence.
Source: BizBuySell; IBBA Market Pulse
APA: IBBA. (2025). Market Pulse Survey.
